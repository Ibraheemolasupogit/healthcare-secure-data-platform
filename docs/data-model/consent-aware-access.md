# Consent-aware access

Consent-aware controls are portfolio patterns over Milestone 6 `core_consent` and
`core_research_eligibility`. Approved research access requires an approved research
purpose and governed eligibility metadata. Withdrawn or expired consent denies research
use in the local simulator.

Operational care and financial-control access are not incorrectly governed by research
consent. This documentation is not legal advice and requires organisational information
governance review before production use.
