# Retention and export controls

Retention policies are symbolic metadata for the synthetic portfolio. They do not
implement production legal retention, legal holds or deletion.

Export controls use controlled states: `EXPORT_PROHIBITED`, `AGGREGATED_ONLY`,
`REDACTED_DETAIL`, `APPROVED_DETAIL` and
`SYNTHETIC_UNRESTRICTED_WITHIN_REPOSITORY`. Public publishing and external sharing are
denied by default.
