# Synthetic data policy

Only independently generated fictional records may enter this repository. The generator uses no external API, source extract, real name, address, email, telephone number, employee identity or valid production identifier. Labels and codes use visible synthetic markers.

Synthetic does not mean control-free: fields are classified as synthetic direct identifier, synthetic quasi-identifier, clinical, operational, research, governance or public metadata to rehearse future policy design. Real equivalents of birth date, sex, ethnicity, postcode sector, pseudonym, health events and consent would require stringent lawful-purpose, access, retention and disclosure controls.

Committed samples must be small, reproducible, validated, checksummed and manually inspected. Generated medium/large and negative-test outputs remain ignored. If real or plausibly real data is discovered, stop, restrict access and follow `SECURITY.md`.
